# Ground Transport

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Travel Desk

## Order of preference

1. Public transport
2. Ride-hailing or taxi
3. Rental car
4. Personal vehicle (see `mileage-and-personal-vehicle`)

Preference is guidance, not a rule. Choose the option that is reasonable for
the time of day, the amount you are carrying and personal safety.

## Ride-hailing

Standard service class only. Premium classes are reimbursable only where no
standard vehicle is available within 20 minutes, or where you are travelling
with a client.

## Rental cars

Compact or intermediate class. Decline the rental company's insurance — the
corporate card provides equivalent cover. Refuel before returning; prepaid
fuel is not reimbursable.

## Airport parking

Long stay only, up to 8 days. Beyond 8 days, a return taxi is usually
cheaper and should be used.

## Congestion and clean air charges

Reimbursable when incurred on business travel.
