# Corporate Card

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Eligibility

Employees who travel more than four times a year, or who hold purchasing
responsibility. Request via your line manager.

## Limits

Standard limit GBP 5,000 per statement period. Temporary increases are
approved by your Finance Business Partner and expire after 60 days.

## Statement cycle

Statements close on the last calendar day of the month. Expenses must be
reconciled in the expense tool within 21 days of statement close.

## Overdue reconciliation

At 30 days overdue the card is soft-blocked for non-travel merchants. At 60
days it is suspended and the balance may be recovered through payroll where
local law permits.

## Lost or compromised cards

Report to the card issuer first, then to Finance Operations within 24 hours.
Do not wait for business hours.

## Cash advances

Disabled by default. Enabled on request for travel to locations where card
acceptance is limited.
