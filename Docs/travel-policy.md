# Global Travel Policy

**Version:** 4.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations
**Supersedes:** travel-policy-2024 (v3.2)

## Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors and interns travelling on company
business, in every region. Where a local statutory rule conflicts with this
policy, the local rule wins and Finance Operations must be notified.

## Booking

All travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk (Aviator) unless
the travel desk is unavailable for more than 24 hours, in which case
self-booking is permitted and must be flagged on the expense report.

Book at least 14 days in advance where the trip is known. Bookings made
inside 7 days require line manager approval before booking, not after.

## Cabin class

- Flights with a total scheduled flying time **under 8 hours**: economy.
- Flights **8 hours or more**: premium economy is permitted.
- Business class requires a scheduled flying time of **10 hours or more**
  *and* written VP approval obtained before booking.
- Cabin upgrades purchased personally are never reimbursable, including
  upgrades bought with personal loyalty points where a cash value is claimed.

## Accommodation

See `accommodation-standards`. Nightly caps are set by city band and are
reviewed each January.

## Personal travel attached to a business trip

Permitted. The company pays the cost of the business itinerary only. You must
obtain a comparison quote for the business-only itinerary at time of booking
and attach it to the expense report.

## Rail

Standard class for journeys under 2 hours. First class is permitted for
journeys of 2 hours or more where the ticket is cheaper than the equivalent
flexible standard fare, which is common on advance purchase.

## What is never reimbursable

Fines and penalties, in-flight gambling, spa and minibar charges, lost
personal property, travel insurance purchased separately (the company holds a
group policy), and companion travel.
