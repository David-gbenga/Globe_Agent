# Mileage and Personal Vehicles

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Rates (UK)

| Vehicle | First 10,000 business miles per tax year | Thereafter |
|---|---|---|
| Car or van | 45p per mile | 25p per mile |
| Motorcycle | 24p per mile | 24p per mile |
| Bicycle | 20p per mile | 20p per mile |

Passenger supplement: 5p per mile per colleague carried on the same trip.

## What the rate covers

Fuel, wear, insurance and tax. Do not claim fuel separately. Tolls, congestion
charges and parking are claimed separately and are reimbursable.

## Commuting

Home-to-office mileage is never claimable. Where you travel from home directly
to a client site, claim the lesser of the actual distance and the distance
from the office.

## Insurance

You must hold business-use cover. The company does not insure personal
vehicles used on business.
