# Approval Thresholds

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

Approval is on the **total value of the expense report**, not the individual
line, except where a line-level rule is stated below.

| Report total (GBP) | Approver required |
|---|---|
| 0 – 500 | Line manager |
| 501 – 2,500 | Line manager, then department head |
| 2,501 – 10,000 | Department head, then Finance Business Partner |
| 10,001 and above | Finance Director, then CFO |

## Line-level rules that override the table

| Item | Approver required regardless of report total |
|---|---|
| Business class flight | VP, in writing, before booking |
| Client entertainment above GBP 150 per head | Department head |
| Any single line above GBP 5,000 | Finance Business Partner |
| Accommodation above the city band cap | Finance Business Partner |
| Software or subscription purchase | See `procurement-and-vendors` |

## Self-approval

Never permitted, at any value, including for department heads. If your line
manager is unavailable for more than 5 working days, their manager approves.

## Delegation

Approvers may delegate during planned absence via the expense tool. Delegation
is logged and time-bounded. Verbal delegation is not recognised.
