# Remote Work and Home Office Equipment

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** People Operations

## Home office allowance

GBP 500 on joining, refreshed at GBP 300 every two years thereafter. Covers
desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, lighting and headset.

## What is not covered

Rent, mortgage interest, general household utilities, broadband line rental
(usage is not separable), furniture for shared household use, and consumables.

Broadband is covered only where an employee is contractually home-based and
the connection is upgraded specifically for work; that requires People
Operations approval.

## Ownership

Equipment purchased under the allowance belongs to the company above GBP 200
per item and must be returned on leaving. Below GBP 200 it transfers to the
employee.

## Co-working spaces

Day passes are reimbursable up to 8 days per month for home-based employees,
with line manager approval. Ongoing memberships require department head
approval.

## Accessibility adjustments

Not funded from the allowance. Contact People Operations; these are funded
centrally and separately.
