# Policy Exception Requests

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## What an exception is

A request to reimburse something the policy would otherwise refuse. Common
cases: accommodation above the city band cap, a late claim, a booking made
outside the travel desk, a missing receipt above GBP 250.

## How to raise one

In the expense tool, open the report and choose "Request exception". State
what rule you are asking to set aside, why, and what it costs. Attach
evidence.

Exceptions are raised **against a specific expense report**. You cannot raise
a general or forward-looking exception.

## Who decides

| Value at stake (GBP) | Decision maker |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000 | Department head |
| 1,001 – 5,000 | Finance Business Partner |
| Above 5,000 | Finance Director |

## Turnaround

Five working days. Urgent requests are flagged by the department head, not by
the requester.

## Precedent

Exceptions do not create precedent. A repeated exception is a signal that the
policy needs changing — raise that with Finance Operations separately.

## Audit

Every exception is retained for seven years with the requester, the decision
maker, the reason and the amount.
