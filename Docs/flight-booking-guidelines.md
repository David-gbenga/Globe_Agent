# Flight Booking Guidelines

**Effective:** 2025-03-01 | **Owner:** Travel Desk

Practical guidance for booking flights. Where this document and the Global
Travel Policy disagree, the more recently dated document governs.

## Choosing a fare

Aviator shows a "policy compliant" badge. Book the cheapest compliant fare
within GBP 75 of the lowest available fare on your chosen routing.

Do not book non-refundable basic economy fares for trips more than 30 days
out — the change fees usually exceed the saving.

## Cabin class

Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Loyalty programmes

You may keep personal airline and hotel loyalty points earned on business
travel. You may not choose a more expensive carrier in order to earn them.

## Seat selection

Standard seat selection fees are reimbursable up to GBP 40 per leg. Extra
legroom and exit row fees are reimbursable only on flights of 6 hours or more.

## Changes and cancellations

Cancel through Aviator as soon as a trip is called off. Change fees are
reimbursable when the reason is business-driven; they are not when the reason
is personal.
