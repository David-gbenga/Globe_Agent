# Accommodation Standards

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Nightly caps by city band

| Band | Example cities | Cap per night (GBP) |
|---|---|---|
| A | London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo, Zurich, Singapore | 260 |
| B | Berlin, Madrid, Chicago, Toronto, Dublin, Sydney | 195 |
| C | Manchester, Lisbon, Warsaw, Austin, Kraków | 150 |
| D | All other locations | 120 |

Caps are room rate plus local taxes, excluding breakfast where breakfast is
itemised separately.

## Exceeding a cap

Permitted where the conference or client site has a room block at a higher
rate, or where no compliant room is available within 5km. Attach evidence and
raise an exception request (see `exception-requests`).

## Long stays

Stays of 14 nights or more should use serviced apartments where available.
The travel desk will source these; do not book directly.

## Room sharing

Never required. Employees may share voluntarily.

## Airbnb and short-let platforms

Permitted in bands C and D only, and only for stays of 3 nights or more.
Entire-property bookings only; shared-host stays are not permitted for
insurance reasons.
