# Client Entertainment

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Definition

Meals, events or hospitality where at least one non-employee attends.
Employee-only meals are team events, not client entertainment.

## Limits

Up to GBP 150 per head requires line manager approval only. Above GBP 150 per
head requires department head approval in advance.

## Required record

Every claim must list every attendee by name and organisation, and state the
business purpose in a sentence. "Client dinner" is not a business purpose.

## Anti-bribery

Never offer hospitality to a public official without Legal sign-off. Never
offer hospitality during a live tender with that party. Gifts above GBP 50 in
value must be recorded in the gifts register regardless of direction.

## Team events

Budgeted separately by department. Not covered by this policy.
