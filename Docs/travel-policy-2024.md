## Cabin class

- Economy for all flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy permitted for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class permitted for flights of 9 hours or more with line manager
  approval.

## Advance booking

Book at least 7 days in advance.

## Rail

Standard class only, all journeys, no exceptions.

## Accommodation

Flat cap of GBP 180 per night worldwide.
