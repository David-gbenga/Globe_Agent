# Per Diem and Meal Allowances

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

The company operates a **receipted meal allowance**, not a true per diem.
You claim what you spend, up to the daily ceiling.

## Daily meal ceilings

| Band | Ceiling per day (GBP) |
|---|---|
| A | 75 |
| B | 60 |
| C | 45 |
| D | 35 |

City bands are defined in `accommodation-standards`.

## What the ceiling covers

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and non-alcoholic drinks for the traveller only.
Meals for clients or colleagues go under client entertainment, not here.

## Alcohol

One drink with an evening meal is reimbursable within the daily ceiling.
Alcohol is not reimbursable at breakfast or lunch.

## Travel days

Full ceiling applies on any day involving more than 4 hours of travel,
regardless of departure time.

## Meals provided

Where a meal is provided by a conference, airline or hotel, reduce your claim
for that day by GBP 15 for breakfast and GBP 25 for lunch or dinner.
