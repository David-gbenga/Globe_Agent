# Expense Submission Deadlines

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Deadline

Submit within 60 days of the date the expense was incurred. For a multi-day
trip, the clock starts on the last day of the trip.

## Late claims

| Age of claim | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Up to 60 days | Normal processing |
| 61 – 90 days | Requires department head approval with a written reason |
| Over 90 days | Not reimbursable except by Finance Director exception |

## Year end

All claims for the financial year must be submitted by 10 April. Claims
arriving after that date are charged to the new financial year.

## Leavers

Final claims must be submitted before the last working day. Claims from
former employees cannot be processed through payroll and require a manual
supplier payment set up by Finance Operations.

## Status meanings

`draft` — not yet submitted, editable by the owner.
`submitted` — awaiting approval, no longer editable.
`approved` — approved, queued for the next payroll run.
`rejected` — returned with a comment; edit and resubmit.
`paid` — settled.
