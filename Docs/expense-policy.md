# Expense Policy

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance Operations

## Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for every claim of GBP 25 or more. Card
statements are not receipts. For claims under GBP 25 a description is
sufficient.

Missing receipt? Complete a missing receipt declaration in the expense tool.
More than three declarations in a rolling quarter triggers a review by your
line manager.

## Currency

Claim in the currency you paid. The expense tool converts at the card
settlement rate, or at the ECB rate on the transaction date for cash.

## Personal spend on the corporate card

Repay within 14 days of the statement date. Repeated personal use may result
in card withdrawal.

## Categories

Travel, accommodation, meals, client entertainment, ground transport,
equipment, software, training, other. "Other" requires a description of at
least 20 characters.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run, cut-off is the 20th of the
month.
